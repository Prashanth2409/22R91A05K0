The Label.

Not a brand. A uniform for everybody who never fit the mould.

FLIQ started the way most good things in Hyderabad do — loudly, on the street, between an auto stand and an irani café. We were tired of choosing between men’s and women’s racks, tired of thin fabric pretending to be fashion, tired of waiting for "seasons".

So we built the opposite: one rack, one fit, cut oversized, built heavyweight. Tees you can wear four days straight. Hoodies that outlive trends. Cargos with pockets for everything the city asks you to carry.

FLIQ is unisex by default, Hyderabad by birth, and yours by choice. Wear the city.

What we stand on

Four rules / No exceptions
01

Unisex by default

One fit, sized S–XXL. No pink tax, no shrink-it-and-pink-it. If it looks good, it’s yours.

02

Heavyweight everything

240 GSM tees. 400 GSM hoodies. Fabric with a spine — because thin fabric is a choice, and it’s the wrong one.

03

City-made

Designed, printed and packed in Hyderabad. Every drop is numbered like an issue — when it’s gone, it’s gone.

04

No seasons

We drop when it’s ready, not when a calendar says so. Follow the page or hear about it late.
